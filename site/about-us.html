The Founding Story: 27 July 1914

At the dawn of Monday, the 27th July, 1914, the inception of Jhaveri Bros was initiated by Late Shri Shyamdas Jhaveri with a trailblazing venture and keen foresight to benchmark excellence in the growing sector of exclusively designed gifts, novelties, and trophies at a time when Corporate India was taking shape.

During its formative decades, Jhaveri Bros. managed direct luxury imports from England and Germany, establishing an early monopoly in specialized gift articles. The firm premiered in the city of Mumbai with exclusive silver sports trophies, medals, shields, and presentation cups.

Despite historic challenges spanning two World Wars, economic depressions, currency crises, and civic turbulences, Jhaveri Bros. stood as an unshakeable citadel of commercial integrity and dependable service.

Family Stewardship Across Generations

The baton of Late Shri Shyamdas Jhaveri's vision was later taken ahead by his youngest son, Late Shri Durlabh Jhaveri. Under his stewardship, the company continued its pioneering path:

  • Early 1960s: Became the first in India to import the world-renowned Montblanc brand, operating as its sole retailer in the country then.
  • 1981: Acquired the official distributorship of CROSS writing instruments, renowned globally with a lifetime mechanical guarantee, becoming its first distributor in Mumbai.

Currently, the tradition of this heritage business with its core vision is administered by his enterprising daughter, Seyjhal D. Jhaveri, who has served as Director of the company for over 24 years. Under her leadership, Jhaveri Bros. retains its foundational commitment: to delight customers through exquisite design, dependable service, and personal attention.
