CUSTOMIZATION ATELIER

Craftsmanship & Personalization Services

Transforming awards, shields, and corporate gifts into cherished keepsakes through nine verified precision engraving, etching, and finishing techniques.

2 SAMPLES • CONTINUOUS LOOP
PROCESS 01

Laser Engraving

Laser engraving and laser marking is a high-precision method of utilizing laser beams to engrave or mark an object. The impact of laser engraving has been particularly pronounced for specially designed laser-friendly materials, including laser-sensitive polymers, coated metals, and novel alloys.

Material Suitability: Polymers, Acrylic, Wood, Laser-Sensitive Metal Alloys, Coated Metals.
Enquire for Laser Engraving →
PROCESS 02

Pantograph Engraving

A traditional mechanical method where key letters or intricate designs are traced using a master stylus connected to a pantograph arm. Followed by a cutting tool that pushes and cuts the lettering or image cleanly into the metal surface. This process is uniquely suited to engrave curved surfaces such as silver-plated bowls, presentation salvers, and championship cups.

Material Suitability: Silver-plated bowls, Cups, Brass Salvers, Curved Metal Articles.
Enquire for Pantograph Engraving →

PROCESS 03

Etching

Metal, glass, or stone surfaces are coated with a protective resist layer and cut into the unprotected areas using controlled chemical or acid action to produce crisp, permanent text and illustrations that will never fade.

Material Suitability: Brass, Stainless Steel, Architectural Glass, Granite & Marble.
Enquire for Etching →
4 SAMPLES • CONTINUOUS LOOP
PROCESS 04

Embossing

Carving or moulding a design on a surface so that it stands out in tactile relief. Embossing is the process of creating a three-dimensional image or pattern in fine metal blanks, silver coins, and specialized presentation paperboards.

Material Suitability: Silver Coins, Copper & Brass Medals, Presentation Boxes.
Enquire for Embossing →
2 SAMPLES • CONTINUOUS LOOP
PROCESS 05

Screen Printing

A printing technique involving a precision mesh stencil through which opaque inks are transferred onto flat materials. Enables vibrant multi-colour corporate logos and crisp typography across plaques, acrylic panels, and presentation boxes.

Material Suitability: Polished Wood, Metal Plates, Acrylic Panels, Optical Crystal.
Enquire for Screen Printing →
PROCESS 06

Sand Blasting

A method of treating and etching the surface of crystal, glass, or polished stone by spraying it with fine abrasive media propelled by compressed air. Creates an elegant frosted, translucent finish with clean dimensional contrast.

Material Suitability: Optical Crystal, Bevelled Glassware, Granite & Marble Bases.
Enquire for Sand Blasting →
PROCESS 07

3D Engraving

Using advanced multi-axis engraving techniques to create images that appear three-dimensional. Portraits and monuments can be converted into lifelike models suspended inside crystal blocks or standing out in relief.

Material Suitability: Internal Crystal Blocks, Solid Brass Medallions.
Enquire for 3D Engraving →
PROCESS 08

Sticker Printing • Identification

Adhesive metallic, coated foil, and acrylic markers used as identifying labels attached to trophy pedestals, medals, and gift articles to display event names and participant categories.

Material Suitability: Trophy Pedestals, Tournament Medals, Presentation Boxes.
Enquire for Identification Markers →
2 SAMPLES • CONTINUOUS LOOP
PROCESS 09

Foil Engraving

A specialized printmaking and finishing technique in which gold or silver leaf foil is affixed to an object or presentation plaque and engraved upon to create an opulent reflective gleam.

Material Suitability: Solid Hardwood Plaques, Certificate Folders, Acrylic Trophies, Leather Mounts.
Enquire for Foil Engraving →
