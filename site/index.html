OUR HERITAGE

A Tradition That Has Spanned Generations

At the dawn of Monday, 27 July 1914, Late Shri Shyamdas Jhaveri established Jhaveri Bros. in Mumbai with keen foresight to benchmark excellence in exclusively designed gifts, novelties, and trophies at a time when Corporate India was taking shape.

Premiering with exclusive sports trophies, medals, shields, and silver cups, the business stood firm through world wars and economic shifts as an unshakeable citadel of commercial integrity. The baton was subsequently carried forward by his youngest son, Late Shri Durlabh Jhaveri, and is currently administered by director Seyjhal D. Jhaveri.

Historical Milestones (1914 – Present)

Explore verified milestones documenting more than a century of leadership in the Indian awards and corporate gifting sector.

1914
Foundation at the Dawn of Modern Corporate India

On Monday, 27 July 1914, Late Shri Shyamdas Jhaveri established Jhaveri Bros. in Mumbai, pioneering the supply of exclusively designed gifts, novelties, medals, shields, and silver sports trophies.

1920s – 1950s
International Sourcing & Commercial Integrity

The firm developed a premier reputation for specialized luxury gift items and managed direct imports from England and Germany, withstanding world wars and trade depressions through uncompromised standards.

Early 1960s
Montblanc Sole Retailer in India

Jhaveri Bros. became the first in India to import the prestigious Montblanc brand, serving as its sole retailer then and establishing an enduring reputation for luxury writing instruments.

1981
CROSS Distributorship in Mumbai

Late Shri Durlabh Jhaveri acquired the distributorship of CROSS writing instruments, renowned globally for lifetime guarantees, becoming the first official distributor in Mumbai.

1982
World Hockey Cup & Himalayan Rallies

Manufactured major international and national trophies, including commissions for the 1982 World Hockey Cup, the Himalayan Rallies, and the prestigious Sanjay Gandhi Trophy.

Present Day
Contemporary Stewardship by Seyjhal D. Jhaveri

Administered by director Seyjhal D. Jhaveri for over two decades, the company continues its founding tradition, pairing bespoke craftsmanship with modern precision engraving for India's leading organizations.
